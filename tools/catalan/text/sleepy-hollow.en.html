In Sleepy Hollow, a Dutch valley on the edge of the Hudson where everyone believes in ghosts, Ichabod Crane works as a schoolmaster. He is a tall, awkward, gluttonous and superstitious man who has set himself a goal: to marry Katrina Van Tassel, the heiress of the richest farmer in the region. The drawback is Brom Bones, a strong, boastful young bachelor fond of heavy jokes who is also trying to court her. Even so, the main obstacle walks at night along the valley paths: the spectre of a headless soldier who rides towards the cemetery looking for him.

Washington Irving's most famous piece and the starting point of the Headless Horseman is La llegenda de Sleepy Hollow (1820). It is a horror story that works at the same time as a comedy: a text of ironic spirit, full of food, music and village gossip, whose outcome the reader must decipher for themselves. Ideal for an October night, it is read in a single sitting.

This translation is expressed in standard, contemporary Catalan that preserves the mocking tone of the narrator, while keeping the names, the miles and the yards of the original text.
