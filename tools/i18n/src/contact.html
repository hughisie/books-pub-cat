Should a book fail to arrive, show up damaged, or the digital download not come through, email us with your order number and we will resolve the issue. Damaged copies are swapped without question and returning the damaged item is unnecessary.

Corrections are sincerely welcomed, they are implemented, and we publicly acknowledge them. This field has true specialists, and we prefer to learn from them rather than remain silently incorrect.
