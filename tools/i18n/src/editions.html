A one-line guide to deciding

For academic study of al-Būnī, take the Francis volume. That is genuine research; our title does not claim to be.

For a carefully chosen introduction from a publisher with strong credentials in the field, the selected translation fits the bill.

For the entire text, every one of the four parts, complete with reproduced square diagrams for close inspection, we filled that gap. Our edition is the one to order.

Watch out for the budget options

Low-priced paperbacks with eye-catching covers, slim page totals and no named translator are common in this field. Many are machine-rendered from an incomplete source and stretched out. The tell-tale signs are easy to spot: the translator is unnamed, no manuscript source is identified, there is no introduction, and the page count is nowhere near what a work of this scale demands. Run those three checks on any purchase in this area, our own title included.
