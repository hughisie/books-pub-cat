For an operation of this size, that support means a great deal. Here is what to expect now.

Digital edition purchasers

The PDF and EPUB files are being sent to the email address provided during checkout, typically within minutes. If fifteen minutes pass without delivery, please inspect your spam folder and then get in touch, and we will resend them manually.

Paperback purchasers

Each copy is produced to order, so printing may take two to four days, followed by dispatch. Most locations see delivery within 7 to 12 days. A tracking number will arrive by email as soon as your parcel leaves us.

Should the book arrive in a damaged state, take a photograph and send it to us. We will arrange a replacement without asking you to return the original.

In the meantime

The supporting pages are open to all and worth exploring before the book arrives: the life of al-Būnī, the study of letters, and the construction of the squares.

One favour. If you enjoy the book, a review on Amazon carries more weight than any paid promotion we could run. If you do not, contact us directly and let us put things right.
