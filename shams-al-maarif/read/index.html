How the translation was made

The English rendering was produced from the 546 scanned folios of the Cairo edition of 1347 AH (1928), issued by the Ḥusayniyya Egyptian Press, using the volume preserved at the American University of Beirut. The task was undertaken with sophisticated AI translation technology, which handles each printed Arabic page image individually. Any passage flagged by the software as uncertain was later checked by hand prior to its inclusion in the finished work.

This is an aged print from the early part of the twentieth century. Across eighty-nine brief sections, the inking is damaged or light, leaving no definitive interpretation intact, which amounts to roughly one part in two thousand of a work exceeding 570,000 words. Every such instance is flagged at its exact location rather than concealed, and a reproduction of the original page appears alongside the English rendering, allowing the reader to observe precisely what the translator had before them.
